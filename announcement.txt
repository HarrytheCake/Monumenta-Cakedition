&bThe Architect's Ring: The Wanderer's Fortune&r
After over three years of planning and development, Team
Monumenta is incredibly excited to announce the release of a new
dungeon coming to Region 3,
&bThe Wanderer's Fortune&r! A strange new stall has appeared in
Port Manteau, where a crier speaks of unclaimed Gold in a casino
far off the coast of the Ring...
Not all is as it seems in this gambler's paradise though, and
you'll have to risk it all for a chance at the jackpot. Do you
dare test your luck in a place such as this, for fortune only
favors the bold...
The dungeon is set to launch during our standard content window
of <t:1788624000:F>, and is accessible to those who have
completed the Brown Wool Dungeon!
To accompany the dungeon's release, a new quest is also being
added,
&bFighting Gold&r, which will be available immediately following
this week's update! Requiring the completion of &oMechs
Machina&r, the quest will be completable up until the point that
you discover the dungeon lobby...
