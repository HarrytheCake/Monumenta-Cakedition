# CakeBook

CakeBook is a guide book and a small wiki that lives in your game. It reads the books your modpack or resource packs bring, keeps your own notes, and searches all of them at once. Everything stays on your computer.

## Opening it

- The **book button** next to *Back to Game* in the pause menu
- The **book button** to the right of your inventory
- A key: set *Open guide book* in Options → Controls (it has no key at first). Yours now: {key:key.cakebook.open}
- The command `/cakebook`

The book opens where you left it.

## Finding your way

- The **contents** on the left list every book. Click a book to open its first page; click the arrow to fold it.
- **Search** looks through every page and every note. Each word you type must appear. Press Ctrl+F to jump to the search box.
- **Back and forward**: the `<` `>` buttons, your mouse's side buttons, or Backspace for back.
- **Scrolling**: mouse wheel, arrow keys, Page Up / Page Down, Home / End.
- Blue underlined text is a link. Links to websites ask before they open.

## Commands

All of them run on your side only and are never sent to the server.

- `/cakebook`: open the book
- `/cakebook open <page>`: open a page, e.g. `/cakebook open help/notes` (press Tab for suggestions)
- `/cakebook search <words>`: open the book with a search
- `/cakebook note <title>`: start a new note
- `/cakebook reload`: read the books again after changing them
- `/cakebook folder`: open the folder for your own books
