### Delve Changes:
Stat Multiplier has been split into two separate multipliers:
Basic Stat Multiplier and Elite Stat Multiplier. These apply to
basic mobs and elites/bosses respectively.
• Basic Stat Multiplier now includes a chance to spawn an
additional mob (per wave).
• Elite Stat Multiplier's speed and damage are slightly higher
than the basic multiplier, health increases tremendously.
The two features above only begin scaling past 12 pts and caps
at 25pts.
-# Note that Elite DMG & HP scale past 25pts if in a
depths/zenith shard at half effectiveness.
Legionary has been reworked as the original modifier has been
merged with the stat scaling system.
• Spawners have a chance to trigger Legionary. A legion of
(6/8/10/12/14) mobs spawn within a 24 block radius. Legionary
mobs are (10/20/30/40/50)% faster when more than 8 blocks away.
• Transcendent Missiles tweaked to fire every 1.5s (previously
burst of 3).
• Transcendent Duplication mob count is now uncapped.
### New Experimental Modifier: Deep Delve
• Worth 0 pts, allows health and damage multipliers to scale
