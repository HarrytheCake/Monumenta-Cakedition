### Region One Rework Part 1:
&oThis is part one of two of our planned Region one early game
revamp. Part two will be completely rebuilding the swamp,
overworld deaths, earlygame quests, and more&r
&nDream Sequence&r
• Upon first login, players will now land in an introduction
sequence replacing The Julia Room ™
• The Julia Room ™ has been obliterated
• Upon completion of the sequence, players will select a class
before entering the Tutorial (Temple to Kaul)
&nTutorial and Temple to Kaul&r
• The Tutorial and Temple to Kaul have been rebuilt!
&oNote: This rebuild aims to maintain the layout and spirit of
the prior version&r
• The player now has a class for their clear of tutorial
• All mobs have had their stats adjusted for class gameplay
• The Masked Man cutscene has had its audio and visual effects
completely rewritten
• The ability bar is now on by default if UMM is not installed
&nThe Alchemy Labs&r
• The Alchemy Labs has been entirely rebuilt from the ground up!
&oNote: The opening section of the Alchemy Labs rebuild has been
designed to fit a new final entrance location in the Swamp, set
to release in Part 2 of the rework&r
• An instance can only be opened once a week, to match other
dungeons
• Alchemy Labs now has five new rare items found in loot rooms
&oNote: Expect more rares and changes to their obtainment in
part 2&r
• A new utility unique can be found within the dungeon to help
with potion management
• Alchemy Labs can now be delved upon completion of the
monument, including a new challenge variant and delve bounty
• Alchemy Labs delves now give an epic crafting material, which
can be used for an infusion and an epic, or purchasing twisted
strands
• Old completions and race times have been legacied
• New food items can be found within the dungeon
• Added two discoveries in Alchemy Labs&nEarlygame Progression
Rework&r
• The Fallen Menagerie and Arcane Rivalry no longer grant skill
points upon first completion. Instead, the player begins with 3
skill points, and earns a fourth on the completion of mage's
legacy
• Each class begins with four unlocked skills, and four locked
skills
• Two skills are unlocked upon first completion of The Fallen
Menagerie, and Two more on first completion of Arcane Rivalry
&nItem Changes&r
• Bandit's & Crimson Mage Sets (T2) are now both armor, from
agility
• Tempered Ironwood Blade (T2) is now armor, instead of agility
• Significantly increased the expected rate of Scythes from T1
T2 and T3 chests.
• Peasant's scythe Unbreaking 1 -> 2
• Ruffian's Scythe Unbreaking 2 -> 3
• Salubric Scythe Unbreaking 0 -> 3
