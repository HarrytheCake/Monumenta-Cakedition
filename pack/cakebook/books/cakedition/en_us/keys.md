# Keys

The keys below are what *your* game uses right now. Change any of them in Options → Controls; the search box there (from Controlling) finds a key by name.

## Hotbar

Installs from before 2.4.1 started with hotbar slots 1 and 2 on R and F; new installs keep Minecraft's 1 and 2.

- Hotbar slot 1: {key:key.hotbar.1}
- Hotbar slot 2: {key:key.hotbar.2}

## Monumenta

- Lock or unlock the selected inventory slot (UMM): {key:unofficial-monumenta-mod.keybinds.lock_slot}
- Calculator, on the Plots shard only (UMM): {key:unofficial-monumenta-mod.keybinds.toggleCalculator}
- Player stats (Monumenta Addons): {key:key.mma.playerstats}
- Player HP indicator on/off (Monumenta Addons): {key:key.mma.togglePlayerHpIndicator}
- Copy charm stats (Monumenta Addons): {key:key.mma.cz_copy_stats}
- Move UMM's HUD elements: {key:njols-ui-framework.key.editHud}

## Map

- Open the world map: {key:gui.xaero_open_map}
- Map settings: {key:gui.xaero_open_settings}
- Quick manual confirmation: {key:gui.xaero_quick_confirm}

With the optional minimap, it starts with: Z enlarge the minimap, U waypoint list, J new waypoint (B in installs from before 2.4.1), keypad + quick waypoint, Y minimap settings.

## Seeing better

- Zoom (hold): {key:zoomify.key.zoom}
- Second zoom: {key:zoomify.key.zoom.secondary}
- Brightness (gamma) on/off: {key:key.gamma_utils.gamma_toggle}
- Brightness up / down: {key:key.gamma_utils.increase_gamma} / {key:key.gamma_utils.decrease_gamma}
- Health and food bars on/off (AsteorBar): {key:asteorbar.key.toggle_overlay}

## Other

- Auto jump on/off (Amecs): {key:key.amecs.toggle_auto_jump}
- Click through signs and item frames on/off: {key:key.clickthrough.toggle}
- Sounds Be Gone! settings: {key:soundsbegone.config}
- This book: {key:key.cakebook.open}
- Monumenta Wiki (CakeWiki), also over an item: {key:key.cakewiki.open}
- Peek at the chat while playing (CakeChat, hold): {key:key.cakechat.peek}
- Hide or show the chat (CakeChat): {key:key.cakechat.hide}
- Search the chat: Ctrl+F in the open chat

## Keys that do two things

Installs from before 2.4.1 started with several actions on **B**: auto jump, the player HP indicator, Sounds Be Gone! settings, and (with the minimap) new waypoint. The middle mouse button both picks blocks and shows player stats. If one of them gets in your way, give it another key.
