# CakeWiki

Reads the Monumenta Wiki (monumenta.wiki.gg) inside the game.

- Press {key:key.cakewiki.open}, or type `/wiki <page>`.
- In your inventory or a chest, hover an item and press {key:key.cakewiki.open} to open that item's page.
- The search box suggests pages as you type. ◀ ▶ go back and forward, ⟳ reloads, ↗ opens the page in your browser.
- **Bookmarks:** ☆ (or Ctrl+D) keeps the page, ☰ lists your bookmarks.
- Pages are kept on your PC for a while, so pages you open again load at once.
- With CakeText Translator, pages are shown translated; names (mainly the linked ones) stay in English.

The wiki's text is under CC BY-SA 4.0; every page shows its title, link and license.
