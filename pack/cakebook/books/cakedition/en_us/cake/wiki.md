# CakeWiki

Reads the Monumenta Wiki (monumenta.wiki.gg) inside the game.

- Press {key:key.cakewiki.open}, or type `/wiki <page>`.
- In your inventory or a chest, hover an item and press {key:key.cakewiki.open} to open that item's page.
- The search box suggests pages as you type. ◀ ▶ go back and forward, ⟳ reloads, ↗ opens the page in your browser.
- **Bookmarks:** ☆ (or Ctrl+D) keeps the page, ☰ lists your bookmarks.
- Pages are kept on your PC for a while, so pages you open again load at once.
- With CakeText Translator, pages are shown translated; names (mainly the linked ones) stay in English.
- **Other wikis:** outside Monumenta, ⇄ switches between the wikis in `config/cakewiki/sites.json` (the Monumenta Wiki and a Chinese Wynncraft guide to start with). `/wiki <site>:<page>` opens a page on any of them. Bookmarks are kept per wiki.
- **"Verify that you are human":** with a VPN or game accelerator, the wiki sometimes shows Cloudflare's check, which CakeWiki can't pass. It then explains why and offers **Retry** and **Open page in browser**. If you install the optional MCEF mod yourself (check that your server allows it), **Verify in game browser** lets you pass the check once inside the game.
