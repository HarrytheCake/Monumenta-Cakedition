# CakeText Translator

*Optional: on only if you picked it during the first install.*

Shows the game's English text in Chinese (Simplified or Traditional): chat, item tooltips, titles, the action bar, the scoreboard and boss bars.

- **Human translations first.** Text that has one is shown with a book icon.
- **The rest by a machine** (chip icon), if you turn one on: a translation model on your PC, a translation server on your PC or home network (for example Ollama with Hunyuan-MT-7B), or an online service with your own key. The mod downloads no model itself.
- **How it shows:** the translation with the original on hover, both, or the original with the translation on hover. In tooltips, hold Alt for the original.
- **Player chat:** only the message is translated, never names, and only by an engine on your PC or home network.
- Signs, books and shulker boxes are never translated.

Settings: `/cttranslate` or Mod Menu.
