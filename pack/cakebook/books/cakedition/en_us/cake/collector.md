# CakeText Collector

*Optional: on only if you picked it during the first install.*

Collects the English text the server shows (with where it was seen) into files on your PC, so translators can translate it.

- It never collects player chat or player names.
- Nothing is uploaded: the files stay in `config/caketext/`. Exports for the translation website are in `config/caketext/exports/crowdin/`.
- Off in singleplayer by default.

Settings and status: `/ctcollect` or Mod Menu. `/ctcollect export` writes the export files now; `/ctcollect off` pauses collecting.
