# CakeFixes

Small fixes for bugs in Minecraft and other mods that hurt on Monumenta. Each one has a switch in Mod Menu → CakeFixes; turned off, the game behaves exactly as without the fix.

- **Team freeze fix** (on): Monumenta sometimes removes a player from a team they aren't on. Minecraft then builds a full crash report, which froze the game for 0.1–0.5 s each time, often in dungeons. CakeFixes skips those removals.
- **AppleSkin fix** (on): AppleSkin's food bar made your processor wait for the graphics driver every frame. CakeFixes answers its question without the wait.
- **Banner draw distance** (off): banners are drawn up to 64 blocks away and cost a lot in dungeons full of them. Choose 16, 24, 32 or 48 blocks for more FPS; banners further away aren't drawn.

The settings screen shows how often each fix has helped since you started the game.
