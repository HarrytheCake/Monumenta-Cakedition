# CakeBook

This book, and your own notes.

## Opening it

The book button in the pause menu or next to your inventory, {key:key.cakebook.open}, or `/cakebook`. Mod Menu opens it too.

## Reading

The contents are on the left; the search box finds words in every page and note. Links in the text open other pages.

## Your notes

Under **My notes** you can write notes of your own. While writing, **Insert location** adds where you are (shard, area and coordinates), and you can link to pages or other notes. Notes are files in `config/cakebook/notes/` on your PC; pack updates never change them.

## Commands

- `/cakebook open <page>`: open a page
- `/cakebook search <text>`: search
- `/cakebook note <title>`: start a new note
