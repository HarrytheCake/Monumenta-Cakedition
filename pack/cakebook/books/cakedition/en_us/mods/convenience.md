# Convenience

- **Mouse Tweaks**: faster inventory handling: drag to move or split stacks, scroll to move items.
- **Item Highlighter**: marks items you just picked up.
- **Shulker Box Tooltip**: see what's inside a shulker box by hovering it.
- **ToolTipFix**: long tooltips no longer run off the screen.
- **ClickThrough**: click through signs and item frames to open the chest behind them ({key:key.clickthrough.toggle}).
- **Zoomify**: zoom ({key:zoomify.key.zoom}).
- **Controlling**: a search box in Controls, to find a key by name.
- **Amecs**: modifier keys (Ctrl, Shift, Alt) in key combinations, and more than one action per key.
- **Remove Reloading Screen**: resource packs reload in the background while you keep playing.
- **Language Reload**: switching the game language is quicker.
- **Screenshot to Clipboard**: screenshots (F2) are copied, ready to paste.
- **Sounds Be Gone!**: turn off single sounds you don't like ({key:soundsbegone.config}).
- **CakeBook**: this book and your notes. Made for this pack: see [CakeBook](../cake/book.md).
- **CakeWiki**: the Monumenta Wiki inside the game ({key:key.cakewiki.open}, also over an item). Made for this pack: see [CakeWiki](../cake/wiki.md).
