# Monumenta

- **Unofficial Monumenta Mod (UMM)**: client features made for Monumenta, among them knowing which shard and area you're in, slot locking ({key:unofficial-monumenta-mod.keybinds.lock_slot}) and a calculator on the Plots shard.
- **Monumenta Addons**: more Monumenta tools: a player stats view, HP indicators that colour players and mobs by their health, and copying charm stats.
- **Monumenta Item Dictionary**: look up Monumenta items in game.
- **Monumenta resource pack** (MonumentaRP): the server's textures and models, kept up to date by Cakedition Updater.
