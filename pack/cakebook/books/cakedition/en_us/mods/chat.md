# Chat

- **CakeChat**: the pack's chat, made for this pack.
  - Several chat windows: drag one by its tab bar, resize it from its top-right corner.
  - Tabs for Monumenta's channels (Global, LFG, Trade, 中文, Guild, Whisper). Clicking one joins that channel; All takes you back to your own.
  - Unlimited history, kept when you switch shards or reconnect. Repeated lines are merged, with optional timestamps.
  - In the open chat: **Ctrl+F** searches, right-click a message to copy it. Hold {key:key.cakechat.peek} to look at the chat while playing.
  - Settings: `/cakechat` or Mod Menu. If you used ChatPlus, CakeChat offers to import its windows and tabs.
- *Optional:* **CakeText Translator**: translates the game's English text into Chinese, with human translations first and a machine engine for the rest. Made for this pack.
- *Optional:* **CakeText Collector**: collects the game's English text (never player chat or names) into files on your PC, for translators. Nothing is uploaded. Made for this pack.
