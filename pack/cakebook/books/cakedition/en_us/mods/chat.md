# Chat

- **ChatPlus**: a movable and resizable chat with tabs, copying and searching messages, and more.
- **More Chat History**: keeps many more chat lines.
- **Don't Clear Chat History**: the messages you sent (up arrow) survive leaving and rejoining.
- *Optional:* **CakeText Translator**: translates the game's English text into Chinese, with human translations first and a machine engine for the rest. Made for this pack.
