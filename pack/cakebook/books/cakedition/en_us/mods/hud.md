# On-screen information

- **AsteorBar**: slim health, food and armour bars, and health bars over mobs ({key:asteorbar.key.toggle_overlay} turns them off and on).
- **AppleSkin**: how much hunger and saturation food gives.
- **TslatEntityStatus**: a mob's health and status when you look at it.
- **BetterF3**: a clearer F3 screen.
- **Better Ping Display**: your ping as a number in the player list ({key:key.playerlist}).
- **AdvancementInfo**: more detail on what an advancement needs.
- **Xaero's World Map**: a full-screen map of everywhere you've been ({key:gui.xaero_open_map}).
- *Optional:* **Xaero's Minimap (Fair-Play)**: a minimap in the corner. The Fair-Play edition leaves out features servers may count as unfair, like cave mode.
