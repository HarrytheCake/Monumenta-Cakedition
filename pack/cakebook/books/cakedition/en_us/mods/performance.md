# Performance

These make the game faster and smoother. You don't need to touch them.

- **Sodium**: a much faster rendering engine; its Video Settings replace the normal ones.
- **Sodium Extra**: more Video Settings pages (animations, particles, details, FPS display).
- **Reese's Sodium Options**: a tidier layout for Sodium's Video Settings.
- **Nvidium**: an even faster renderer for NVIDIA graphics cards; on other cards it stays off.
- **Lithium**: faster game logic.
- **C2ME**: faster chunk loading.
- **ModernFix**: faster start-up, less memory and many bug fixes.
- **ImmediatelyFast**: faster drawing of text, HUD and similar things.
- **More Culling**: skips drawing what you can't see.
- **Enhanced Block Entities**: faster chests, signs, beds and similar blocks.
- **FeyTweaks**: faster signs and beacons.
- **Experimental Performance** and **Faster Random**: small speed-ups in several places.
- **Packet Fixer**: fixes some connection problems and timeouts.
- **Log Begone**: keeps useless lines out of the game log.
