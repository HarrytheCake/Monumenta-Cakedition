# Mods in the pack

The pack has about 70 mods. Most work on their own; a few have settings worth knowing. Mod Menu (the **Mods** button on the title screen and in the pause menu) lists every mod, and its settings button opens that mod's options.

- [Monumenta](monumenta.md)
- [Performance](performance.md)
- [Visuals](visuals.md)
- [On-screen information](hud.md)
- [Chat](chat.md)
- [Convenience](convenience.md)
- [Tools and libraries](tools.md)

Mods marked *optional* are off unless you picked them during the first install.
