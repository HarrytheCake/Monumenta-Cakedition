# Visuals

- **CIT Resewn**: custom item textures. The Monumenta resource pack uses them to show what an item is, so keep it on.
- **Entity Model Features** and **Entity Texture Features**: custom mob models and textures from resource packs, used by Monumenta's mobs.
- **LambDynamicLights**: torches and other light sources light up the area while held or dropped.
- **Model Gap Fix**: removes thin gaps in block and item models.
- **No Night Vision Flickering**: night vision fades out gently instead of flashing.
- **Gamma Utils**: a brightness boost for dark places ({key:key.gamma_utils.gamma_toggle}).
- **Dark Loading Screen** and **Loading Backgrounds**: darker loading screens with Paperbox's pictures.
- **Capes**: shows capes from OptiFine, LabyMod and other cape mods.
- **Translations for Sodium**: Sodium's menus in more languages.
- *Optional:* **Iris Shaders** with **Complementary Reimagined**: shaders for nicer light and shadows. They cost a lot of FPS.
