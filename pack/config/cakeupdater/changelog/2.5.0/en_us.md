**Added**
- CakeFixes, our new mod of small fixes for Monumenta. It stops the freezes of up to half a second that came every few seconds in some dungeons, and stops AppleSkin's food bar from slowing down every frame.
- Particle Core: particles outside your view are no longer drawn, and particle lighting is worked out once instead of every frame. Particles you can see look the same.
- In a dungeon test, the two together raised the average from 89 to 144 FPS and the slowest moments from 8 to 44 FPS.
- CakeFixes can also hide banners farther than a distance you choose (Mod Menu → CakeFixes). This is off unless you turn it on.
