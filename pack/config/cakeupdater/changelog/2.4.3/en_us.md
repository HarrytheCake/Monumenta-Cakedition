**Changed**
- New installs show AsteorBar health bars over other players instead of over yourself. The bars are smaller, show within 32 blocks, and your own health bar border turns red when your health is low.
- New installs that turn on the optional LambDynamicLights start with lighter settings: only the light you hold yourself, in its fastest mode. With every light source on it cost a lot of FPS in boss fights with many players.
- Existing installs keep their own settings.
