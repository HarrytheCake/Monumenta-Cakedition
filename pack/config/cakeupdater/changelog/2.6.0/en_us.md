**Added**
- Moving from another pack? Cakedition Updater can bring your settings along: options and keybinds, UMM, Monumenta Addons, Xaero's waypoints and map, your server list and your ChatPlus tabs. It offers this in chat, or type /cakeupdate import. Everything it replaces is backed up first.
- CakeChat's Guild tab now joins your guild's channel by itself, using your guild tag from the tab list.
- The guide has a new chapter, "Cakedition's own mods", with a page for each of our mods. Chat links to it on first launch and after updates.
- Moe_block's Chinese guide book "Monumenta 中文翻译" (classes, specializations, enchantments, The Celestial Zenith), used with permission.

**Changed**
- Performance and compatibility settings now update with the pack, so fixes reach existing installs too. Your keys, HUD and look are never touched.
- The ChatPlus import moved from CakeChat into Cakedition Updater's import.
- Cakedition Updater has an icon in Mod Menu.
