**New**
- CakeChat replaces ChatPlus, More Chat History and Don't Clear Chat History: chat windows with tabs for Monumenta's channels (click a tab to join it), unlimited history kept across shard switches, Ctrl+F search, right-click to copy, hold Left Alt to peek at the chat. If you used ChatPlus, it offers to import your windows and tabs. Settings: /cakechat.
- Entity Culling and Ixeris: more FPS in busy places like the Market, and smoother mouse and keyboard.
- Optional: CakeText Collector collects the game's English text into files on your PC for translators (never player chat or names; nothing is uploaded).

**Changed**
- LambDynamicLights is now optional and off by default: it costs a lot of FPS in busy places. To keep it, tick it if this update asks about optional mods, or later click "Optional mods..." in the window that installs pack updates.
- Cakedition Updater 1.5.0: shows changelogs like this one, and warns once if the game has too little or too much memory.
- CakeText Translator 0.3.0 (optional): online translation services with your own key, and translation of CakeWiki pages.
- The CakeBook guide covers the new mods and has a new "Java and memory" section: we recommend Java 21.
- New installs start with faster settings (ImmediatelyFast sign text buffering, Nvidium extra render distance 0, UMM texture spoofing off, spark background profiler off). Existing installs keep their own settings.
