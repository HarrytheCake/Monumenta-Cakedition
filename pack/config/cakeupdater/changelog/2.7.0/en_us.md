**Added**
- Added CakeUI, our new mod with a HUD editor: move, resize, hide and restyle the pieces of your HUD, and add your own text, bars or images. Open it in a world from Mod Menu → CakeUI → Edit HUD, or with the Edit HUD button in the chat. Your edits are only for you; without them the game looks as before.
- Added a CakeUI page to the guide's "Cakedition's own mods" chapter.

**Changed**
- Updated CakeWiki to 0.3.0: when the wiki asks you to verify that you are human (common with a VPN or game accelerator), CakeWiki explains why and offers Retry, Open page in browser, or verifying in the optional MCEF in-game browser if you install it yourself.
- Let CakeWiki show other wikis outside Monumenta (⇄ button, /wiki site:page), with bookmarks per wiki. Its key stays K.
- Updated CakeChat to 0.4.0: chat windows are locked so you can't move them by accident. Type /cakechat edit (or use its settings) to move or resize them.
- Fixed CakeChat's tab reset, which could leave tabs blank; tabs it blanked are repaired.
- Updated Cakedition Updater to 1.8.0: an automatic backup of your server list, settings and waypoint lists every 30 minutes of play.
- Improved the import from another pack: tick or untick each line; it adds Xaero's waypoints to yours instead of replacing them, and frees the mouse while its folder window is open.
- Updated CakeBook to 0.1.2: the book button next to your inventory moved to the left, out from under the status effects.
